## 🚀 About Me 
I'm a data professional from Harare, with a Diploma in Data Science from TelOne Centre for Learning. I enjoy turning messy, real-world data into dashboards, forecasts and tools that people actually use, whatever the industry.

My background mixes IT support, data work and web administration at Zimbabwe Open University, where I also helped rebuild and manage the university website on WordPress. I've worked in sales and customer service at OutsourceSpot Zimbabwe, and I run my own mobile device and IT equipment resale business. That gives me a practical, business-first view of data: I've built analytics on my own sales and stock numbers, not just practice datasets.

My projects cover **retail demand forecasting, inventory analytics and predictive modelling**, and I've applied machine learning to problems in healthcare as well. I'm heading toward **BI development and data engineering**, building reliable pipelines and dashboards that help organisations in any sector, from retail and education to finance and public services, make better decisions.

Outside tech, I'm into gym training and playing football
